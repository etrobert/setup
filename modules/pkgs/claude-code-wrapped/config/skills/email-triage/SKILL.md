# Email triage

Use `himalaya` for email processing.

Go through the inbox 5 emails at a time: suggest an action for each, and act
only after Étienne says go. Act on emails covered by the rules below without
asking.

## Archive without asking

- Calendar invites and invites to past events
- Bookings, appointments and travel once their date has passed
- Order and shipping notices for delivered orders
- Urban Sports Club emails
- Receipts and €0 invoices, without saving them
- Newsletters older than 1 week
- Terms and privacy updates
- Login codes and security alerts

## Portal notifications

Arbeitsagentur and TK notifications: check the portal against `~/sync/doc/mail`,
then archive.

## Job offers

Check whether an offer is exceptional and flag it. Otherwise reply that Étienne
works as a freelancer and would be happy to talk about working together that
way, on LinkedIn for InMails, by email otherwise.

## Attachments

Move attachments worth keeping into `~/sync/doc` and point any related calendar
events to the files there.
